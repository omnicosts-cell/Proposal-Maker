A NOTE FOR BRADLEY

Why we're reaching out

Hi Bradley,

AZD has been designing homes and buildings since 1990. Your work has won more than 120 design awards and reaches from Michigan to Canada and the Caribbean. Work like that needs clean, careful drawings, and a lot of them.

As a project manager, you're the one who gets those sets out the door on time. When three jobs hit at once, something has to give. We'd like to be the extra hands you call on those weeks.

Brixen Consultancy is a drawing team. We make architectural, structural, civil and MEP drawings in Revit and AutoCAD for U.S. design firms and builders. Our own engineers draw and check every sheet. You keep the design, the stamp and the client. We just help get the sheets done.
